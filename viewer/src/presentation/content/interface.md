## Interface for your own body or lens

> Draft. Not locked until a test print confirms it.

Two joints hold the camera together: where a body meets the film back, and where a lens module meets a body. A body or module that keeps to the values below should fit the rest of the system. Distances run from the film plane toward the front of the camera. Heights run from the centre of the gate.

### Film back to body

The film back owns the film plane, and every body registers against its front face, the datum face. The body hangs on a hook rail along the top of the film back and swings in until it sits on the datum face, which carries a velvet light seal. A latch hook at the bottom then clicks over a steel pin of {{value:latch_pin_diameter}} diameter. The latch pulls the body tight but does not position it, because the datum face does that. The cartridge and take-up chambers beside the gate are clearance only, so a body must not touch them.

### Body to lens

A lens or pinhole module screws into an M42 thread with {{value:m42_pitch}} pitch on the front of the body. The female thread comes from a bought rear lens cap glued into the front plate, since printed threads are unreliable. On the simple body the mount seat sits {{value:m42_seat_to_film}} from the film plane. Whatever goes in the mount must throw an image circle of at least {{value:image_circle}}, the diagonal of the frame.
